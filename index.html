Before you buy nothing

What do I actually receive?

After a confirmed purchase: a personalised, numbered digital receipt as proof you took part, which you can download and share. Nothing else. Checkout isn’t open yet, so nobody has bought anything and no real receipts exist.

Is it worth anything?

No. It has no resale value and it isn’t a financial product. It’s a joke you can keep.

Is this an investment?

No. Buying NOTHING gives you no equity, ownership, returns or guaranteed future products. If a separate project ever launches, early supporters may be offered something, such as selected products at cost. That isn’t promised, and it doesn’t exist today.

Do I have to appear on the Wall?

No. Anonymous is the default. Being listed is your explicit choice, and showing an Instagram link is a separate explicit choice. The Wall isn’t open yet.

Why would I share my Instagram?

Only if you want to be seen with your nothing. We don’t promise followers, clicks or reach.

Is checkout active?

No. The prices shown are the planned ones, and nothing can be bought yet.

What happens to my information?

Right now this site stores nothing. The receipt preview lives in your browser tab, and the contact form sends nothing until a submission endpoint is connected. When checkout opens, payment will be handled by a payment provider, and we’ll say exactly what we keep before launch.

Can I ask to be removed from the Wall?

That’s the intention: once the Wall exists you’ll be able to ask through the contact form, choosing “Wall listing or removal request”. The exact process will be confirmed before launch.

What about refunds?

To be confirmed before anything is for sale.
